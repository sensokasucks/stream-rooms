# Log Cabin sounds

`loon_1.ogg`, `loon_2.ogg`, `loon_3.ogg`: common loons calling on a lake, played now and then by
`log_cabin_room.gd` `_run_loons()` in Cabin: Lakeside (more often at dawn, dusk and night, never
in rain or a storm).

They are short cuts (10 to 13.5 seconds, mono, 32 kHz, low rumble filtered out, faded in and
out, levelled) from a recording in Yellowstone National Park's sound library, which the US
National Park Service publishes as public domain ("The files available here were recorded in the
park and are in the public domain"):
https://www.nps.gov/yell/learn/photosmultimedia/sounds-commonloon.htm

- Common loons making a series of tremolo calls on Yellowstone Lake (NPS & MSU Acoustic Atlas /
  Jennifer Jerrett, 2016-09-13). Cuts from 0:41.5, 1:03.5 and 1:29.

`gull_1.ogg`: western gulls calling, played now and then by day by `log_cabin_room.gd`
`_run_ocean()` in Cabin: Ocean (a little higher or lower each time). A cut (mono, 32 kHz,
filtered, faded, levelled) of the US National Park Service Sound Gallery's "Western Gull"
recording (Golden Gate National Recreation Area, California; credit NPS). The gallery says "The
files are in the public domain and may be downloaded":
https://www.nps.gov/subjects/sound/gallery.htm, https://www.nps.gov/subjects/sound/sounds-western-gull.htm

The surf and the foghorn in Cabin: Ocean are generated in code (`_fill_surf`, `_make_foghorn`).
