# Route 22 sounds

`coyote_1.ogg`, `coyote_2.ogg`, `coyote_3.ogg`: coyotes howling, played at night by
`r22_desert.gd` `howl()` (the Coyotes tick box on the Room tab).

They are short cuts (7 to 15 seconds, mono, 32 kHz, faded in and out, levelled) from recordings
in Yellowstone National Park's sound library, which the US National Park Service publishes as
public domain ("The files available here were recorded in the park and are in the public
domain"): https://www.nps.gov/yell/learn/photosmultimedia/sounds-coyote.htm

- `coyote_1`, `coyote_2`: a group of coyotes howling at night near Blacktail Pond
  (NPS and MSU Acoustic Atlas / Jennifer Jerrett, 2015).
- `coyote_3`: a group of coyotes howling along the Gibbon River (NPS / Neal Herbert, 2014).
